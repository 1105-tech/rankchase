Privacy Policy

RANKCHASE – Last Updated: 28 August 2026

RANKCHASE respects the privacy of users who use this website. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and how it is handled when you use our services.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the ranking service, you may provide information including your response-sheet URL, category, gender and horizontal reservation details.

Information that may be available from the submitted response sheet can include candidate-related examination information such as name, roll number, examination centre, examination date, examination time, post, score and other information required to generate the analysis.

2. Purpose of Processing

The information submitted by users is processed to generate examination score analysis, ranking estimates, normalized scores, statistical comparisons and related reports.

3. Data Storage

Information required for the operation of the ranking service may be stored in the website's backend database and associated infrastructure.

Candidate information submitted through the service is intended to be retained only for the period required for operating the service and its related analysis. Data may be automatically deleted according to the website's configured data-retention process.

4. Data Retention

Candidate records are configured to be automatically deleted after approximately 30 days from their creation, subject to the operation of the website's automated deletion process.

5. Analytics

RANKCHASE may use Google Analytics to understand website usage, traffic and general user interaction. Analytics information may be processed by Google according to Google's applicable policies.

6. Third-Party Infrastructure

RANKCHASE may use third-party infrastructure and service providers, including Cloudflare and GitHub, for hosting, deployment, website operation, code management and backend functionality.

These third-party services may process technical information as necessary to provide their respective services and are subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Response Sheet URLs

Users should submit only response-sheet URLs that they are authorized to use. Do not submit another person's private information without appropriate authorization.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures may be used to protect information processed through the website. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Sharing

RANKCHASE does not intend to sell candidate information. Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate, maintain, secure or improve the website and its services.

10. User Responsibility

Users are responsible for reviewing the information they submit and for ensuring that they have the necessary rights or authorization to submit any response-sheet URL or personal information.

11. External Links

The website may contain links to external websites or services. RANKCHASE is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites. Users should review the privacy policies of those websites separately.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website, services or applicable requirements. The updated version will be published on this page.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the handling of information, please contact us through the Contact Us page.
